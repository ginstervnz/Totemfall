## Asset Creators & AI Utilization
A project of this scale relies on a harmonious blend of programming and artistic vision. We extend our heartfelt thanks to the independent artists and creators whose visual assets breathed life into our code, specifically highlighting the outstanding pixel art contributions from Reaktori, Batareya, and Atelier Pixerelia. 

Additionally, in our pursuit of a highly immersive audiovisual experience, we formally disclose the use of Artificial Intelligence technologies. Advanced AI generation tools were responsibly utilized to compose the musical tracks, design the ambient soundscapes, and render the high-resolution background imagery present in the game. These tools allowed us to achieve a polished, atmospheric presentation that perfectly complements our underlying gameplay mechanics.
